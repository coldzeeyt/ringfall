How to play

  • Move

    Mouse, drawing pad pen, arrow keys or a gamepad stick. The ring around your circle is where notes get caught.

  • Tap notes

    Get under the yellow note and hit (Space) as it touches the ring.

  • Hold notes

    Keep hit held and stay under the ribbon until it ends. Let go early or slide off and it breaks.

  • Drawing pad

    Hover the pen to move, touch the tablet to hit. Pen buttons can be bound in Settings.

  • Pause

    Esc, or Start on a gamepad.
